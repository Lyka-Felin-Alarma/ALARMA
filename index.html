About Siargao

Siargao is a beautiful island located in Surigao del Norte, Philippines. Famous for its world-class waves, crystal-clear waters, palm-lined roads, and peaceful island atmosphere, Siargao is a favorite destination for travelers and nature lovers.

Beautiful Spots in Siargao

Cloud 9

Cloud 9 is one of Siargao's most famous attractions. It is internationally known for its powerful waves and is a popular destination for surfers.

Sugba Lagoon

Surrounded by lush limestone formations, Sugba Lagoon offers turquoise water, peaceful scenery, swimming, kayaking, and paddleboarding.

Magpupungko Rock Pools

During low tide, natural rock pools appear along the coast. Visitors can swim in the clear water while enjoying the surrounding coastal scenery.

Naked Island

Naked Island is a small sandbar surrounded by clear blue water. Its simple and peaceful landscape makes it a perfect place to relax and enjoy the ocean.

Guyam Island

Guyam Island is a small tropical island covered with palm trees. It is a relaxing stop for island hopping, swimming, and taking photographs.

Coconut Road

Siargao's palm-lined roads are among the island's most recognizable sights. A motorcycle ride through the coconut groves offers beautiful views of the island's natural landscape.

Experience Siargao

Watch a video and listen to relaxing island sounds to get a glimpse of the beauty and atmosphere of Siargao.

Travel Tips

  • Explore Nature

    Take time to explore the beaches, lagoons, islands, and coconut forests.

  • Try Surfing

    Beginners can take surfing lessons while experienced surfers can enjoy Siargao's famous waves.

  • Check the Tide

    Check tide schedules before visiting attractions such as the Magpupungko Rock Pools.

  • Protect the Environment

    Keep the island clean, avoid single-use plastics, and respect local communities and marine life.
